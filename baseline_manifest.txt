Frozen empirical scope
----------------------

This snapshot freezes the available Hercules audit data and supporting scripts.
It should be treated as the baseline evidence package for the final report.

The frozen study covers:

1. Extraction of citations from Hercules-generated draft judgments.
2. Linkage of each generated draft to its closest exported semantic-search
   record.
3. Verification of citation status against the linked retrieval record and the
   frozen Qdrant corpus registry.
4. Manual authority-group review of citation correctness.
5. Manual holding-accuracy and reasoning-grounding annotation on a validation
   sample.
6. Automated screening-verifier predictions and validation against adjudicated
   human labels.
7. Exploratory comparison between real and synthetic input documents.

Methodological boundaries for final report
------------------------------------------

The original proposal included controlled experiments varying retrieval depth,
prompt design, and model choice. Those experiments are not included in this
frozen empirical snapshot. The final report should describe this as a revised
scope and explain that the submitted analysis focuses on auditing the frozen
Hercules output/retrieval corpus rather than experimentally modifying Hercules.

The verifier should be described as an automated screening verifier. In the
current run, authoritative holding-source text was not supplied, so automated
holding-accuracy predictions are mostly `unclear`. Substantive holding-accuracy
findings should therefore be based on the manual/adjudicated annotation fields,
not on the verifier's lexical holding prediction alone.

The real-versus-synthetic comparison is exploratory because the data are a
convenience sample and not a random sample of all Hercules generations.
